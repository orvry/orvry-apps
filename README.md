# Orvry Apps legal pages

Upload these folders to the root of your GitHub Pages repository:

- `privacy-policy/index.html` → `https://your-domain/privacy-policy/`
- `terms-of-service/index.html` → `https://your-domain/terms-of-service/`

Before publishing:
1. Confirm that the privacy policy accurately describes the website's actual hosting, email, form, analytics, cookies, and retention practices.
2. Confirm the legal operator's full name and business contact details.
3. Complete the governing-law and court-location placeholders in the Terms of Service.
4. Create separate privacy notices and terms for RallyClub and MarieMart if those products collect user information or provide accounts, transactions, or other services.
5. Have the documents reviewed for the laws that apply to the business and its users.

These are general starting drafts, not legal advice.
