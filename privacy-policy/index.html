Privacy Policy

Learn how Orvry Apps handles information when you visit our website or contact us about a project.

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026

Before publishing: This draft assumes the Orvry Apps marketing website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, does not use advertising trackers or analytics, and uses a mailto contact form that opens the visitor’s email application. Update this policy if you add analytics, cookies, embedded services, a server-side form, or other data collection. Replace any operational details that are not accurate.

1. Who we are

Orvry Apps (“Orvry Apps,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) creates websites, web applications, and digital products. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us about a project, or otherwise communicate with us.

For privacy questions, contact support@orvry-apps.com.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, company or organization, and any contact details you choose to provide.
  • Project information: the type of service you are interested in, budget range, project description, timelines, and other details you include in your inquiry.
  • Communications: information contained in emails or other messages you send to us.
  • Basic technical information: information that may be included in hosting or server logs, such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and request time, as handled by our hosting provider.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the general contact form or email unless we have specifically requested it through an appropriate secure channel.

3. How we collect information

You provide information when you email us, submit a project inquiry through a form or service we make available, or otherwise communicate with us. Our website may also generate basic technical logs through its hosting infrastructure.

The contact form on this website may open your device’s email application with a prepared message addressed to us. When you send that message, your email provider processes and transmits it. The message is not submitted to Orvry Apps merely by opening the email application.

4. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communicate with you.
  • Understand, scope, and discuss potential projects.
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, and service agreements.
  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • Keep appropriate business records and meet legal obligations.
  • Protect our rights, users, systems, and business from misuse or security incidents.

5. Legal bases and purposes

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to respond to your request or take steps before entering a contract, perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate business interests such as operating and securing our services, or based on your consent where consent is required.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers: providers that host our website, operate our email, or support our business operations, subject to appropriate safeguards where required.
  • Professional advisers: legal, accounting, or other advisers who need the information to provide services to us.
  • Authorities or other parties: where disclosure is required by law, legal process, or is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, reorganization, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable law.

7. Hosting, cookies, and analytics

This website is intended to be deployed as a static website using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure may process technical information in connection with delivering and securing the website. Please review GitHub’s own privacy information for details about its processing.

Our current website design does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. If we add analytics, embedded content, marketing tools, or other technologies that collect information, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by law.

8. External websites and our products

Our website may link to third-party websites, including RallyClub and, when available, MarieMart. Those services may have their own privacy policies and data practices. This policy applies to the Orvry Apps marketing website only; it does not automatically apply to information collected within those separate products or on third-party websites. Please review the applicable privacy notice on each service.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will take steps required by that law.

10. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to inquiries, managing potential or active projects, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the nature of the information and the circumstances.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no website, email transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.

To make a request, email support@orvry-apps.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain information where permitted or required by law.

12. Children’s privacy

Our general website and business inquiry process are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Changes take effect when posted unless otherwise stated.
